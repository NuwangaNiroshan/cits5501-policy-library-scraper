Task 3 – Live-Site Request Observation

URL:
https://uwa-policy.cits5501.arranstewart.io/policy/

Date:
14 October 2026 (exact time not recorded)

Purpose:
Perform a small controlled check for obvious request limiting without placing
unnecessary load on the live policy website.

Command:

for i in {1..5}; do
  echo "Request $i"
  curl -L -sS -o /dev/null \
    -w "HTTP=%{http_code} time=%{time_total}s size=%{size_download}\n" \
    https://uwa-policy.cits5501.arranstewart.io/policy/
  sleep 2
done
